**STRATA 0.3.1** is an early beta of STRATA, an 8-track techno performance groovebox for the
**Sonicware SmplTrek**, built as an app for [Enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga). It
overhauls the GRAIN machine and fixes how samples and machines load, on top of
[0.3.0](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/releases/tag/v0.3.0). It runs on the same Enaga 0.2.0
firmware, so updating from any earlier STRATA is one file.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early beta.** Nothing new in 0.3.x has been through the unit walkthrough yet, and its CPU
> cost on the unit is not measured. Expect rough edges, and please report them. Installing
> third-party firmware is at your own risk, and neither Enaga nor STRATA is made, supported or
> endorsed by Sonicware.

## Requirements

- A **Sonicware SmplTrek** whose SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**. The Lofi-12 XT and CyDrums
  are the same board, but STRATA has not run on either.
- **Enaga 0.2.0, exactly**, the same firmware as every STRATA release so far. The zip carries it,
  byte for byte the file from
  [Enaga's v0.2.0 release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.2.0).
- An SD card, and a **second SD card** with an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware to go back.

## What is new

**GRAIN, overhauled.** A cloud is about 6 dB louder at the same LVL (it used to sit 11 dB under
its own sample), PIT plays whole semitones, and SIZ reaches 680 ms. Every GRN knob shows its
unit (`151MS`, `X4.3` grains in the air, `HOLD`, `D15`, `OCT25`), so you can dial a cloud in by
eye.

**GRAIN's ASR and OSC pages.** ATK, SUS and REL give a cloud a real envelope: with SUS up it
swells, holds for the step's LEN or while a pad is held, and fades over REL. SCN moves the read
head through the sample while the note sounds, up to twice real time, which is a time-stretch at
any pitch. PSP sprays each grain's pitch, from a slight detune to half the grains an octave up
for a shimmer. All at 0 is the machine as it was.

**Eight GRN factory sounds:** CLOUD, PAD, FREEZE, SHIMMR, DRIFT, STRTCH, STUTTR and CHOPS. They
leave POS and PIT alone, so they work on whatever sample the track holds. On a GRN track, OK in
EDIT opens the SOUNDS list and OK in MACHINE the sample browser.

**Switching machines starts clean.** A track switched to another machine loads that machine's
default sound (the first in its SOUNDS list; CLOUD on GRN, the whole sample on SMP). The knobs
used to keep their positions, so the old machine's values leaked into the new one. FX, LFO and
ARP stay.

**A picked sample plays whole.** A file picked in the browser on a SMP track now plays from its
first frame to its end: STA 0, LEN 127, SLC 1 and DEC on hold. It used to keep the track's
start, slice and decay, so it could start part way in and fade out early.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.1/CHANGELOG.md). Every feature and how sure
we are of it on the unit is in
[docs/FEATURES.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.1/docs/FEATURES.md).

**How to play it:** the [user manual](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.1/docs/MANUAL.md),
[CONTROLS.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.1/docs/CONTROLS.md) and the

thins GRAIN.

## Install

**From any earlier STRATA:** copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` over the old one. The
firmware stays, and your projects and kits open as they are.

**New install:** first check that SYSTEM INFO shows BOOT 1.0.17, a second card holds an official
`SmplTrek.bin`, holding **HOME (F4)** at power-on reaches BOOT MODE, and the unit runs on the power
adapter **without batteries**, so a hung image can be power-cycled. Do not skip these: a hung
image leaves the power key dead.

1. Unzip. Copy `SD-CARD/SmplTrek.bin` to the **root** of the SD card.
2. Hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on to reach BOOT MODE, and choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**. This
   installs Enaga 0.2.0.
3. Copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` to the card as `/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA`.
4. Switch on. Enaga starts the last app that ran, or the only one installed. **F1 + POWER** opens
   the Enaga menu. Press PLAY.

Projects are saved under `/ENAGA/DATA/STRATA/` on the card. Put your own WAVs in `/SAMPLES/`, or
use the ones already in the SmplTrek's pool.

**Going back:** install an official SmplTrek update the same way. Neither Enaga nor STRATA writes
the SPI flash or touches the bootloader, so that restores the unit completely, and your projects
stay on the card.

## Known limits

- **Tested on one unit**, with the cl6x build. On the 0.2.0 build (2026-11-22) the load stayed under 30% on the default beat, and MAX peaked at 84.3% in long, heavy play with no clicks or dropouts; 0.3.x has not run on the unit yet. Nothing new in 0.3.x is
  checked on the unit yet. Every other load figure in
  [docs/BUDGET.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.1/docs/BUDGET.md) is from the
  emulator, which models no caches.
- **Older projects' GRN tracks play louder**, about 6 dB, with SIZ a little longer at the same
  knob and PIT on the nearest semitone. Turn LVL down if a cloud now sits too high in the mix.
- **WAVE and FM4 voices** share the budget of **12 POLY voices** with VA POLY, across all
  tracks. That budget and the CPU guard's thresholds are provisional.
- **The performance mixer and the looper are not saved**, and the looper borrows RESAMPLE's
  buffer: RESAMPLE and FREEZE wait while a loop is held. No overdub yet. One effect at a time.
- **A streamed sample** plays forward only: no START, slices or reverse. FREEZE is not saved.
- **MIDI clock, SYNC and USB MIDI** are lightly tested. On Enaga 0.2.0, **unplug USB when driving
  gear from MIDI OUT**: a computer that is not reading the SmplTrek's USB MIDI port stops the DIN
  jack as well.
- **Before 1.0, nothing is promised** about the project file format or the controls.

## Reporting problems

Open an issue on this repository with the pattern, what you did and what you heard. Please do
not contact Sonicware support about STRATA or Enaga. Problems in Enaga itself belong at
[seawaffle/enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/issues).
