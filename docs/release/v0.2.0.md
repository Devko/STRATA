**STRATA 0.2.0** is an early beta of STRATA, an 8-track techno performance groovebox for the
**Sonicware SmplTrek**, built as an app for [Enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga). It adds
held notes on the VA synths, freezing a track to RAM, help for every screen, factory sounds, an
editable sidechain duck, a CPU guard and more to [0.1.0](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/releases/tag/v0.1.0).
It runs on the same Enaga 0.2.0 firmware, so updating from 0.1.0 is one file.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early beta.** This build has run on one SmplTrek, but the features new in 0.2.0 have not been
> through the unit walkthrough yet. Expect rough edges, and please report them. Installing
> third-party firmware is at your own risk, and neither Enaga nor STRATA is made, supported or
> endorsed by Sonicware.

## Requirements

- A **Sonicware SmplTrek** whose SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**. The Lofi-12 XT and CyDrums
  are the same board, but STRATA has not run on either.
- **Enaga 0.2.0, exactly**, the same firmware as STRATA 0.1.0. The zip carries it, byte for byte
  the file from [Enaga's v0.2.0 release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.2.0).
- An SD card, and a **second SD card** with an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware to go back.

## What is new

**Held notes on VA MONO and VA POLY.** A seventh EDIT page, **ASR**: attack (up to 5 s),
sustain, release (up to 8 s) and filter sustain. With SUS up, a note holds while its key, pad,
STEP key or MIDI note is held, or for its step's LEN, up to 64 steps. REC records how long you
held a note into its LEN, and holding a note's STEP and pressing a later one sets its length. At
SUS 0, and in every 0.1.0 project, the VAs sound exactly as before. A new factory pattern,
**A05 PAD**, plays four bars of held chords so you can hear it straight away.

**FREEZE and bounce.** FREEZE in the FUNC menu turns a track into a 1-8 bar loop of its own machine
played from RAM: its filter, mix and sends stay live, and unfreezing is instant. On the A05 pad it
halves the average load (20.3% to 10.1% in the emulator with cl6x). RESAMPLE can now record one
track on its own (B = `TRK 1`-`TRK 8`), dry and mono, onto a file or another track.

**Help for the screen you are on.** Hold FUNC alone and the screen explains itself: what A-D do,
in words, and that screen's gestures. The screen keys turn its pages while FUNC is held.

**Factory sounds.** OK in EDIT or MACHINE on a drum or VA track opens **SOUNDS**: 39 ready-made
sounds (KCK 909, 808, DEEP, PUNCH ...; MNO ROLL, ACID, SUB, PLUCK ...). One OK loads a sound into
that track only, with the beat running, and **AS WAS** puts the track's own sound back.

**An editable sidechain duck.** Three new MIXER pages: how far each track ducks, which track
ducks them, and attack, hold and release. Kits from 0.1.0 open with the old fixed duck.

**Playing.** KEYS on the **pads** (two rows as a piano, with velocity), **NOTE view** (TUN and
PIT read as notes and step a semitone a click), **MACRO LEARN** (push a knob in EDIT to put it on
a macro), **CLEAR PATTERN** in the FUNC menu, STEP lights that show velocity and blink on a
condition, and MACHINE's LEFT/RIGHT now move over the machine grid instead of changing track.

**Under the hood.** A **CPU guard** sheds VA POLY voices and thins GRAIN when the load stays high,
and shows `GUARD1`-`GUARD4` on the bottom line while it does. Every project gets **64 MiB** of
sample memory (was 32), so fewer files stream. Streamed samples now honour LEN. Projects can be
**renamed and deleted** on the PROJECTS page.

The full list, with fixes, is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.2.0/CHANGELOG.md). Every feature and how sure
we are of it on the unit is in
[docs/FEATURES.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.2.0/docs/FEATURES.md).

**How to play it:** the [user manual](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.2.0/docs/MANUAL.md),

## Install

**From STRATA 0.1.0:** copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` over the old one. The firmware
stays, and your projects and kits open as they are.

**New install:** first check that SYSTEM INFO shows BOOT 1.0.17, a second card holds an official
`SmplTrek.bin`, holding **HOME (F4)** at power-on reaches BOOT MODE, and the unit runs on the power
adapter **without batteries**, so a hung image can be power-cycled. Do not skip these: a hung
image leaves the power key dead.

1. Unzip. Copy `SD-CARD/SmplTrek.bin` to the **root** of the SD card.
2. Hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on to reach BOOT MODE, and choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**. This
   installs Enaga 0.2.0.
3. Copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` to the card as `/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA`.
4. Switch on. Enaga starts the last app that ran, or the only one installed. **F1 + POWER** opens
   the Enaga menu. Press PLAY.

Projects are saved under `/ENAGA/DATA/STRATA/` on the card. Put your own WAVs in `/SAMPLES/`.

**Going back:** install an official SmplTrek update the same way. Neither Enaga nor STRATA writes
the SPI flash or touches the bootloader, so that restores the unit completely, and your projects
stay on the card.

## Known limits

- **Tested on one unit**, with the cl6x build. On the 2026-11-22 build the load stayed under 30% on the default beat, and MAX peaked at 84.3% in long, heavy play with no clicks or dropouts. The features new in 0.2.0 are
  not yet checked on the unit. Every other load figure in
  [docs/BUDGET.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.2.0/docs/BUDGET.md) is from the
  emulator, which models no caches.
- **The CPU guard's thresholds** are provisional, and so is the budget of **12 VA POLY voices**
  shared by all tracks. Held ASR notes count against it for as long as they hold.
- **The fast fused VA loop is off** in this build: cl6x rendered it slightly differently from GCC.
  That costs about 1.7 points of load until it is understood.
- **A streamed sample** honours LEN but plays forward only: no START, slices or reverse.
- **FREEZE is not saved**: a reload, NEW or kit recall unfreezes.
- **MIDI clock, SYNC and USB MIDI** are lightly tested. On Enaga 0.2.0, **unplug USB when driving
  gear from MIDI OUT**: a computer that is not reading the SmplTrek's USB MIDI port stops the DIN
  jack as well.
- **Before 1.0, nothing is promised** about the project file format or the controls.

## Reporting problems

Open an issue on this repository with the pattern, what you did and what you heard. Please do
not contact Sonicware support about STRATA or Enaga. Problems in Enaga itself belong at
[seawaffle/enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/issues).
