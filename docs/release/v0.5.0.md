**STRATA 0.5.0** is the **release candidate for 1.0** of STRATA, an 8-track techno performance
groovebox for the **Sonicware SmplTrek**, built as an app for
[Enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga). It adds GEN and EVOLVE, chords recorded note for
note, CLICK and COUNT-IN, choke groups, rolls over LEN, arp depth and 40 more sounds to
[0.4.0](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/releases/tag/v0.4.0), and moves to Enaga 0.4.0. From
here to 1.0 come fixes, measurements and docs, not features: 1.0 is this build with what the
unit test finds fixed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **0.5.0 needs Enaga 0.4.0 firmware**, and the zip **does not include it**. Install Enaga 0.4.0
> from [Enaga's own release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.4.0) first, even
> when you are updating from STRATA 0.4.0, which ran on Enaga 0.3.0. The
> [card builder](https://strata.einblick.dev) does both in one zip.

> [!WARNING]
> **A project saved by 0.5.0 plays its LFOs wrong on 0.4.0** and earlier. 0.5.0 stores the LFO's
> shape and destination as numbers (so new ones can come later without changing old projects),
> and converts every older project, kit, scene and lock when it opens them, so they sound the
> same here. Going back is what breaks: **copy `/ENAGA/DATA/STRATA/` to your computer before
> you update** if you may want to open your projects in 0.4.0 again.

> [!CAUTION]
> **A release candidate.** Nothing new since 0.4.0 has been through the unit walkthrough yet, and
> its CPU cost on the unit is not measured. Expect rough edges, and please report them. Installing
> third-party firmware is at your own risk, and neither Enaga nor STRATA is made, supported or
> endorsed by Sonicware.

## Requirements

- A **Sonicware SmplTrek** whose SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**. The Lofi-12 XT and CyDrums
  are the same board, but STRATA has not run on either.
- **Enaga 0.4.0, exactly**, from [Enaga's v0.4.0 release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.4.0)
  (`enaga-v0.4.0-smpltrek.zip`). STRATA 0.4.0 ran on Enaga 0.3.0; 0.5.0 is built for 0.4.0.
  Enaga's loader checks only a number the versions share, so 0.5.0 on older firmware starts and
  may then misbehave.
- An SD card, and a **second SD card** with an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware to go back.

## Install

**First, the safety checks.** Do not skip these: a hung image leaves the power key dead.

- [ ] SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**.
- [ ] A second SD card holds an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware, so you can restore the unit.
- [ ] Holding **HOME (F4)** while switching on reaches BOOT MODE.
- [ ] The unit runs on the power adapter **without batteries**, so a hung image can be
      power-cycled.

**The easy way: [strata.einblick.dev](https://strata.einblick.dev).** Pick 0.5.0, and your
browser fetches this release and Enaga 0.4.0 from their official GitHub releases, checks both
against their SHA-256, and hands you one zip: copy everything in it to the root of the SD card,
hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on, choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**, and switch on again.

**By hand:**

**Step 1: install Enaga 0.4.0.** Skip it only if the unit already runs Enaga 0.4.0: the Enaga menu
(**F1 + POWER**) shows its version at the top.

1. On [Enaga's v0.4.0 release page](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.4.0),
   download `enaga-v0.4.0-smpltrek.zip` (not the lofi12xt or cydrums one) and unzip it. It holds
   one file, `SmplTrek.bin`.
2. Copy `SmplTrek.bin` to the **root** of the SD card, keeping its name.
3. Put the card in the SmplTrek, hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on to reach BOOT MODE, and
   choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**. Wait until it finishes.

**Step 2: copy STRATA.**

4. Unzip `STRATA-v0.5.0-smpltrek.zip` on your computer.
5. Copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` to the card as `/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA`,
   replacing the old one when you update. Keep the folder names exactly. (Once Enaga runs, its
   menu's USB DRIVE page puts the card on a computer over USB, so the card can stay in the unit.)

**Step 3: play.**

6. Switch on. Enaga starts the last app that ran, or the only one installed; **F1 (FUNC) +
   POWER** opens the Enaga menu, which lists the apps. A first start opens the factory project:
   press **PLAY**.

**Updating from 0.4.0 or earlier:** copy `/ENAGA/DATA/STRATA/` to your computer first (see the
warning above), then step 1 (Enaga 0.4.0), then step 2. Your projects and kits stay on the card
and open in 0.5.0, converted on load.

**Going back:** install an official SmplTrek update the same way as step 1. Neither Enaga nor
STRATA writes the SPI flash or touches the bootloader, so that restores the unit completely, and
your projects stay on the card.

## What is new

**GEN and EVOLVE.** Hold OK in GRID for the track menu. **GENERATE** writes a new part on the
selected track (a rhythm weighted to the beat, and on a melodic track a line in the pattern's
key). **EVOLVE** mutates a track on its own every 1-8 loops: **ORBIT** varies around the part,
**DRIFT** lets the changes add up, and **MIX** draws a different mutation each turn. EVOLVE is
saved with the project.

**Recording:** **chords recorded note for note** on the POLY machines, FM4 and MID, with a
**REC MODE** of REPLACE or ADD; **CLICK** and a **COUNT-IN** of 1 or 2 bars; **note repeat is
recordable** as a roll.

**Sequencing and sound:** **choke groups** (push A-D on MIXER's track pages), **ratchets over
LEN** (rolls from 1/64 to 1/4 with RAMP for build-ups), **arp depth** (VAR, RHYTHM, ACCENT and
RATCHET), **ARPS** (fourteen factory arps), and **40 more factory sounds**, 116 in all.

**Cleaner and cheaper:** FM4 no longer aliases at high IDX, DRIVE no longer pushes the sound off
centre, and a choked track no longer thumps. VA voices, the LONG delay, the HALL reverb, the
hats, WAVE and KICK all cost less, the same sound to the last bit (KICK within -129 dB): the
worst emulator scenario is down from 32.2% in 0.4.0 to 26.3% (rolls over LEN on every track).

**Fixes** for a card pulled while a project opens, kits recalled from the library, DOUBLE and
undo on the playing pattern, recording after a chain step, and COUNT-IN edge cases.

**No gesture moved:** everything 0.4.0 did, it still does the same way.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.5.0/CHANGELOG.md). Every feature and how sure
we are of it on the unit is in
[docs/FEATURES.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.5.0/docs/FEATURES.md).

**How to play it:** the [user manual](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.5.0/docs/MANUAL.md),
[CONTROLS.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.5.0/docs/CONTROLS.md) and the
[cheatsheet](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.5.0/docs/CHEATSHEET.md).

## Load

| | cl6x | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default beat (A01), top line | under 30% | unit, 0.2.0 build (2026-11-22) |
| Heaviest free play, MAX | 84.3%, no clicks or dropouts | unit, 0.2.0 build (2026-11-22) |
| A01 then A04 (`scenario_factory`), peak | 23.2% | emulator, this build |
| GEN and EVOLVE on the default beat (`scenario_gen`), peak | 25.3% | emulator, this build |
| Tracks 6 and 7 on WAVE MONO and WAVE POLY (`scenario_wave`), peak | 25.7% | emulator, this build |
| The ambient pattern, LONG delay and HALL (`scenario_ambient`), peak | 25.1% (0.4.0: 32.2%) | emulator, this build |
| Rolls over LEN on every track (`scenario_ratchetspan`), the worst case, peak | 26.3% | emulator, this build |
| Sample audition streaming from the card (`scenario_audition`), peak | 6.3% | emulator, this build |

All 47 emulator scenarios pass on this build (2026-11-27), with cl6x and GCC-only. The pad gestures
(stutter, LOCK, choke by pad) cannot be played in the emulator, which cannot press pads.

The target is 60% for the worst case. Heavy play with 0.2.0 on the unit went past it without an
overrun, and the CPU guard steps in before 100%: it sheds POLY voices (VA, WAVE and FM4) and
thins GRAIN.

## Known limits

- **Tested on one unit**, with the {{BUILD}} build. {{UNIT_LOAD}} Nothing new in 0.5.0 is checked
  on the unit yet. Every other load figure in
  [docs/BUDGET.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.5.0/docs/BUDGET.md) is from the
  emulator, which models no caches.
- **Projects saved by 0.5.0 play their LFOs wrong on 0.4.0** and earlier (above). From 1.0 on,
  every build reads every 1.0 file.
- **WAVE and FM4 voices** share the budget of **12 POLY voices** with VA POLY, across all
  tracks. That budget and the CPU guard's thresholds are provisional until the unit's readings.
- **The performance mixer, the looper, LOCK and LIVE RND are not saved**, and the looper borrows
  RESAMPLE's buffer: RESAMPLE and FREEZE wait while a loop is held. No overdub yet. One effect at a
  time.
- **A streamed sample** plays forward only: no START, slices or reverse. FREEZE is not saved.
- **MIDI clock, SYNC and USB MIDI** are lightly tested. (Enaga 0.4.0 follows MIDI clock over USB
  MIDI too, and keeps the right tempo after a long pause in the clock.)
- **Before 1.0, nothing is promised** about the project file format or the controls.
