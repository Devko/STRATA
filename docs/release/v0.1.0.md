**STRATA 0.1.0** is the first release of STRATA, an 8-track techno performance groovebox for
the **Sonicware SmplTrek**, built as an app for [Enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga).
Synthesized drums, bass and chords sit next to samples, a step sequencer with parameter locks,
trig conditions and per-track LFOs drives them, and a performance layer (scenes, morph, macros,
mutes, beat repeat, throws, tape stop and mutation) keeps a set moving for an hour.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early release.** Tested on one SmplTrek, on {{RELEASE_DATE}}. Installing third-party firmware
> is at your own risk, and neither Enaga nor STRATA is made, supported or endorsed by Sonicware.

## Requirements

- A **Sonicware SmplTrek** whose SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**. The Lofi-12 XT and CyDrums
  are the same board, but STRATA has not run on either, and this zip has no update file for them.
- **Enaga 0.2.0, exactly.** STRATA is built against Enaga's provisional ABI 0. Enaga's loader
  checks only that number, so another Enaga release could load STRATA and misbehave. The zip
  carries the right firmware, byte for byte the file from
  [Enaga's v0.2.0 release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.2.0).
- An SD card, and a **second SD card** with an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware to go back.

## What is in it

**Sound.** Eight tracks, any machine on any track. KICK, SNARE (with a clap mode), HAT, PERC
(2-operator FM), SAMPLE (one-shot, sliced by note, or cut at its hits with SLC AUTO), GRAIN,
VA MONO (accent, slide, glide) and VA POLY (a chord per step), plus MID, which plays an outboard
synth over MIDI. Every track has drive, a multimode filter, lo-fi, an amp envelope, a sidechain
duck, pan and level, **an LFO** (six shapes, free or synced) and **an arpeggiator**. The sends are
a ping-pong delay and a reverb, each with an ambient mode: a **LONG** delay of up to 12 s with
tape wow, and a **HALL** reverb with shimmer. The master has a DJ filter, a three-band **kill EQ**,
a glue compressor and a limiter. The drum synthesis follows Mutable Instruments Plaits,
reimplemented.

**Sequencer.** Up to 64 steps per track with per-track length, speed and swing, micro-timing,
parameter locks, trig conditions, probability and ratchets. Live and motion recording, a
Euclidean generator, KEYS mode for playing in a scale, and 16 levels of undo. Banks A-H with
queued pattern changes, and a chain that becomes a **song**: each entry can recall a mute state,
and song mode stops at the end.

**Performance.** Seven pads: FILL, REPEAT, MUTE, SCENE A, SCENE B, THROW and MUTATE. Scenes with
a morph between them, four macros, eight mute states, auto-fill, **note repeat** on the pads with
pressure as velocity, **tape stop** and **delay freeze**, seeded mutation you can keep or revert,
and resampling of the master or an audio input to a file or a track.

**Samples and projects.** A **sample browser** on the unit, WAVs at any rate and bit depth, long
samples at 48 kHz, files too big for memory **streamed from the card**, a **project browser** with
save-as, and a kit library shared by every project. Everything lives on the SD card.

**MIDI.** Any track can send its notes to MIDI OUT on its own channel, with a note-off for every
note. MIDI in plays the tracks, sets parameters from CC 16-31 and queues patterns from program
changes. STRATA follows the unit's clock: internal, MIDI clock or SYNC IN, set in the Enaga menu.

**The factory project.** A first start, and every new project, opens a techno kit at 130 BPM in
C minor with four patterns in bank A:

| Pattern | |
| --- | --- |
| A01 GROOVE | Four on the floor, a three-burst clap, 16th hats, an offbeat bass and a dub chord stab |
| A02 GROOVE+ | The same beat developing: trig conditions, a 12-step rim, Cm7 to Abmaj7 |
| A03 BREAK | Kick and bass out, the chord in long dub tails |
| A04 PEAK | Everything up: ratcheted hats, an acid line, a rave stab |

A chain walks them (it ships off; FUNC+HOME, CHAIN, OK turns it on), scenes 2-4 are DUB, DARK and TENSION, eight mute states are
stored, and three macros are ready: B ACID (the bass filter opens), C DUB (chord and rim into the
sends) and D DRIVE (kick and hats). Macro A is left for you, and pad pressure bends it.

**How to play it:** the [user manual](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.1.0/docs/MANUAL.md)
explains the ideas and walks through a first beat on the factory project, and the
[cheatsheet](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.1.0/docs/CHEATSHEET.md) has every control
on one page. Both start with a labelled diagram of the panel, and show every screen.

## Install

Before you start: SYSTEM INFO shows BOOT 1.0.17, a second card holds an official `SmplTrek.bin`,
holding **HOME (F4)** at power-on reaches BOOT MODE, and the unit runs on the power adapter
**without batteries**, so a hung image can be power-cycled. Do not skip these: a hung image
leaves the power key dead.

1. Unzip. Copy `SD-CARD/SmplTrek.bin` to the **root** of the SD card.
2. Hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on to reach BOOT MODE, and choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**. This
   installs Enaga 0.2.0.
3. Copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` to the card as `/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA`.
4. Switch on. Enaga starts the last app that ran, or the only one installed. **F1 + POWER** opens
   the Enaga menu. Press PLAY.

Projects are saved under `/ENAGA/DATA/STRATA/` on the card. Put your own WAVs in `/SAMPLES/`.

**Going back:** install an official SmplTrek update the same way. Neither Enaga nor STRATA writes
the SPI flash or touches the bootloader, so that restores the unit completely, and your projects
stay on the card.

## Known limits

- **Tested on one unit**, with the {{BUILD}} build. MAX, the highest audio load since PLAY, read
  {{MAX_A01}} on A01 GROOVE and {{MAX_A04}} on A04 PEAK. Every other load figure in
  [docs/BUDGET.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.1.0/docs/BUDGET.md) is from the
  emulator, which models no caches.
- **12 VA POLY voices** are shared by all tracks. Past that, the quietest sounding voice is cut
  with a short fade. The number is provisional and will be set from measurements on units.
- **MIDI clock, SYNC and USB MIDI** are Enaga features that STRATA may be the first app to use on
  a unit. Expect rough edges, and please report them.
- **Unplug USB when driving gear from MIDI OUT** on Enaga 0.2.0: a computer that is not reading
  the SmplTrek's USB MIDI port stops the DIN jack as well. Enaga has fixed this for its next
  release.
- **A streamed sample** plays forward from its start on one voice: no start, length, slices or
  reverse.
- **Before 1.0, nothing is promised** about the project file format or the controls: a later
  release may not open a 0.1 project.

## Reporting problems

Open an issue on this repository with the pattern, what you did and what you heard. Please do
not contact Sonicware support about STRATA or Enaga. Problems in Enaga itself belong at
[seawaffle/enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/issues).
