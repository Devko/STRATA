**STRATA 0.3.0** is an early beta of STRATA, an 8-track techno performance groovebox for the
**Sonicware SmplTrek**, built as an app for [Enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga). It adds
a performance mixer and master looper on VALUE, two new kinds of synth (WAVE wavetables and
FM4), an OSC page and a resonance that works over the whole knob on the VAs, and the stock
SmplTrek sample folders to [0.2.0](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/releases/tag/v0.2.0). It runs
on the same Enaga 0.2.0 firmware, so updating from 0.1.0 or 0.2.0 is one file.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early beta.** The features new in 0.3.0 have not been through the unit walkthrough yet, and
> their CPU cost on the unit is not measured. Expect rough edges, and please report them.
> Installing third-party firmware is at your own risk, and neither Enaga nor STRATA is made,
> supported or endorsed by Sonicware.

## Requirements

- A **Sonicware SmplTrek** whose SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**. The Lofi-12 XT and CyDrums
  are the same board, but STRATA has not run on either.
- **Enaga 0.2.0, exactly**, the same firmware as STRATA 0.1.0 and 0.2.0. The zip carries it, byte
  for byte the file from [Enaga's v0.2.0 release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.2.0).
- An SD card, and a **second SD card** with an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware to go back.

## What is new

**A performance mixer on VALUE.** Push VALUE and tap STEP 1-15 to pick one of fifteen master
effects: LP and HP sweeps, HP riser, echo riser, echo freeze, reverb wash, stutter, tape stop,
bass kill, pump, crush, noise riser, flanger, gate and build. VALUE is then its amount on every
screen, and 0 is the dry mix, untouched. Push VALUE + LEFT/RIGHT moves to the next effect, push
VALUE + CLR turns everything off, and a quick tap on VALUE sets the amount back to 0. While VALUE
is held, the STEP keys light up as the sixteen slots.

**A master looper** on push VALUE + STEP 16. It records 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars of the whole mix (LOOP
LEN in the FUNC menu) from the next bar line, and VALUE then crossfades between the live beat and
the loop. Effects picked afterwards play over the loop, and pressing STEP 16 again records a new
take without a gap.

**WAVE MONO and WAVE POLY**, two new machines: the VA voice with a wavetable oscillator. 16
built-in tables of 16 frames each (classic shapes, PWM, sync, formants, FM, harmonics, e-piano,
vowels, growl, pluck, mallets and more), a position that the envelope can sweep (MOT), WRP to
bend the phase and DET for a second, detuned reader. 15 factory sounds. A table is built the
first time a track plays it, so nothing is added to the start-up.

**FM4**, a four-operator FM machine: the eight classic algorithms, feedback on operator 4, an
index envelope faster than the amplitude's, and a detuned second pair. Six voices with chords,
for e-pianos, bells, glass, brass, organs and FM basses. 8 factory sounds (EPIANO, BELL, BASS,
BRASS, ORGAN, MALLET, PAD, GLASS).

**VA MONO and VA POLY, reworked.** An eighth EDIT page, **OSC**: WAV (saw, square or pulse
width), OS2 (off, a detuned second saw for chorus and Reese basses, or a hard-synced pulse),
SUB and DRV (drive into the filter). **RES now works over the whole knob** and self-oscillates
near the top, and LP24 squelches as hard as LP12. Every MNO and PLY factory sound was remade,
and there are six new ones (ACIDSQ, FAT, REESE, SYNC, LEAD, BRASS). Older projects sound a
little different: see the changelog.

**The stock SmplTrek folders in the sample browser.** On a card the stock firmware has used, the
top level adds **POOL** (`/SmplTrek/Pool/Audio`) and **SMPLTREK** (the whole tree, stock projects
included), so your existing SmplTrek samples are one press away. STRATA only reads them.

**Big files stream instead of failing.** Files over 2 MiB load in 2 MiB pieces, which frees
14.5 MiB of memory, and a file that cannot be held whole streams from the card instead of showing
`TOO LONG` or `NO ROOM`.

**Fixes.** A frozen track or a playing loop finds its place on the next bar after a stop and
start, a relocate or a tempo change (it could drift for up to seven bars). A new looper take no
longer cuts the old loop with a click. A refused FREEZE says `FREEZE REFUSED` instead of waiting
forever. WAVE's WRP no longer aliases on high notes.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.0/CHANGELOG.md). Every feature and how sure
we are of it on the unit is in
[docs/FEATURES.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.0/docs/FEATURES.md).

**How to play it:** the [user manual](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.0/docs/MANUAL.md),

The performance mixer and the looper cannot be played in the emulator (it cannot hold VALUE);
the host estimate is about 2 points more at worst, with a loop playing under BASS KILL or BUILD.

The target is 60% for the worst case. Heavy play with 0.2.0 on the unit went past it without an
overrun, and the CPU guard steps in before 100%: it sheds POLY voices (VA, WAVE and FM4) and
thins GRAIN.

## Install

**From STRATA 0.1.0 or 0.2.0:** copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` over the old one. The
firmware stays, and your projects and kits open as they are.

**New install:** first check that SYSTEM INFO shows BOOT 1.0.17, a second card holds an official
`SmplTrek.bin`, holding **HOME (F4)** at power-on reaches BOOT MODE, and the unit runs on the power
adapter **without batteries**, so a hung image can be power-cycled. Do not skip these: a hung
image leaves the power key dead.

1. Unzip. Copy `SD-CARD/SmplTrek.bin` to the **root** of the SD card.
2. Hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on to reach BOOT MODE, and choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**. This
   installs Enaga 0.2.0.
3. Copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` to the card as `/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA`.
4. Switch on. Enaga starts the last app that ran, or the only one installed. **F1 + POWER** opens
   the Enaga menu. Press PLAY.

Projects are saved under `/ENAGA/DATA/STRATA/` on the card. Put your own WAVs in `/SAMPLES/`, or
use the ones already in the SmplTrek's pool.

**Going back:** install an official SmplTrek update the same way. Neither Enaga nor STRATA writes
the SPI flash or touches the bootloader, so that restores the unit completely, and your projects
stay on the card.

## Known limits

- **Tested on one unit**, with the {{BUILD}} build. {{UNIT_LOAD}} The features new in 0.3.0 are
  not yet checked on the unit. Every other load figure in
  [docs/BUDGET.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.3.0/docs/BUDGET.md) is from the
  emulator, which models no caches.
- **WAVE and FM4 voices** share the budget of **12 POLY voices** with VA POLY, across all
  tracks. That budget and the CPU guard's thresholds are provisional.
- **A wavetable is built the first time a track uses it**, in small pieces between screen
  updates. Until it is ready, that WAVE voice plays only its sub. How long that takes on the unit
  is not measured yet.
- **The performance mixer and the looper are not saved**, and the looper borrows RESAMPLE's
  buffer: RESAMPLE and FREEZE wait while a loop is held. No overdub yet. One effect at a time.
- **A streamed sample** plays forward only: no START, slices or reverse. FREEZE is not saved.
- **MIDI clock, SYNC and USB MIDI** are lightly tested. On Enaga 0.2.0, **unplug USB when driving
  gear from MIDI OUT**: a computer that is not reading the SmplTrek's USB MIDI port stops the DIN
  jack as well.
- **Before 1.0, nothing is promised** about the project file format or the controls.

## Reporting problems

Open an issue on this repository with the pattern, what you did and what you heard. Please do
not contact Sonicware support about STRATA or Enaga. Problems in Enaga itself belong at
[seawaffle/enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/issues).
