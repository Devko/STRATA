**STRATA 0.4.0** is an early beta of STRATA, an 8-track techno performance groovebox for the
**Sonicware SmplTrek**, built as an app for [Enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga). It is
the biggest release so far: every screen redrawn as pictures that move with the music, MOD slots,
LFO envelopes, sample audition, step stutter, LOCK, play modes and live randomising, on top of
[0.3.1](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/releases/tag/v0.3.1).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **0.4.0 needs Enaga 0.3.0 firmware**, and the zip **does not include it**. Install Enaga 0.3.0
> from [Enaga's own release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.3.0) first, even
> when you are updating from STRATA 0.3.1, which ran on Enaga 0.2.0. The steps are below.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early beta.** Nothing new in 0.4.0 has been through the unit walkthrough yet, and its CPU cost
> on the unit is not measured. Expect rough edges, and please report them. Installing third-party
> firmware is at your own risk, and neither Enaga nor STRATA is made, supported or endorsed by
> Sonicware.

## Requirements

- A **Sonicware SmplTrek** whose SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**. The Lofi-12 XT and CyDrums
  are the same board, but STRATA has not run on either.
- **Enaga 0.3.0, exactly**, from [Enaga's v0.3.0 release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.3.0)
  (`enaga-v0.3.0-smpltrek.zip`). STRATA 0.1.0 to 0.3.1 ran on Enaga 0.2.0; 0.4.0 does not. Enaga's
  loader checks only a number the two versions share, so 0.4.0 on the 0.2.0 firmware starts and
  then misbehaves.
- An SD card, and a **second SD card** with an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware to go back.

## Install

**First, the safety checks.** Do not skip these: a hung image leaves the power key dead.

- [ ] SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**.
- [ ] A second SD card holds an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware, so you can restore the unit.
- [ ] Holding **HOME (F4)** while switching on reaches BOOT MODE.
- [ ] The unit runs on the power adapter **without batteries**, so a hung image can be
      power-cycled.

**Step 1: install Enaga 0.3.0.** Skip it only if the unit already runs Enaga 0.3.0: the Enaga menu
(**F1 + POWER**) shows its version at the top.

1. On [Enaga's v0.3.0 release page](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.3.0),
   download `enaga-v0.3.0-smpltrek.zip` (not the lofi12xt or cydrums one) and unzip it. It holds
   one file, `SmplTrek.bin`.
2. Copy `SmplTrek.bin` to the **root** of the SD card, keeping its name.
3. Put the card in the SmplTrek, hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on to reach BOOT MODE, and
   choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**. Wait until it finishes.

**Step 2: copy STRATA.**

4. Unzip `STRATA-v0.4.0-smpltrek.zip` on your computer.
5. Copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` to the card as `/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA`,
   replacing the old one when you update. Keep the folder names exactly. (Once Enaga runs, its
   menu's USB DRIVE page puts the card on a computer over USB, so the card can stay in the unit.)

**Step 3: play.**

6. Switch on. Enaga starts the last app that ran, or the only one installed; **F1 (FUNC) +
   POWER** opens the Enaga menu, which lists the apps. A first start opens the factory project:
   press **PLAY**.

**Updating from 0.3.1 or earlier:** do step 1 (Enaga 0.2.0 to 0.3.0), then step 2. Your projects
and kits stay on the card under `/ENAGA/DATA/STRATA/` and open in 0.4.0.

**Going back:** install an official SmplTrek update the same way as step 1. Neither Enaga nor
STRATA writes the SPI flash or touches the bootloader, so that restores the unit completely, and
your projects stay on the card.

## What is new

**Every screen redrawn, and moving with the music.** A common frame (pattern and track badges,
tempo, load, toasts for notices, page tabs along the bottom), and pictures instead of text lists:
GRID's trigs glow as they play, PERFORM shows its macros as gauges over a scope of the output,
EDIT draws each page's sound (a drum's hit, the filter's curve over the live spectrum, envelopes
with a dot riding them, the LFO's shape, WAVE's table as a waterfall, GRAIN's cloud), MIXER is an
eight-strip desk with a spectrum and a vectorscope, and PROJECT maps all 128 patterns.

**Sound design:** two **MOD slots** per track (LFO, ENV, velocity, pad pressure or random moving
any parameter, learnt by turning a knob), **LFO TRIG** (free, retrigger, or one-shot as a second
envelope), **random lock ranges** and **parameter slides** on steps, **SAMPLE holds notes** with
SUS and REL, **one filter** on the VA and WAVE machines with its own decay, velocity opening the
synths' filter, and a cleaner 44.1 kHz resampler.

**Performing:** **step stutter** (hold REPEAT, then a STEP), the repeat rate on the arrows,
**LOCK** (store the moment, jump back in one tap), **play modes** per track (reverse, ping-pong,
random blocks), **LIVE RND**, **REL ALL** and **LEN ALL**, **FEEL** (a groove drift),
**macro learn by turning**, FX GLIDE and momentary pushes on the performance mixer, and the STEP
keys as steps on PERFORM and KEYS.

**Samples and patterns:** **hear a sample before you load it** (push A in the browser),
**bounce to track**, a pattern menu (copy, paste, clear, double), LOAD DEMO, and RESAMPLE takes
that reach the card again.

**Some gestures moved**, so each job has one home (the DJ filter, tape stop and delay freeze are
performance mixer slots now; FUNC + MUTATE is LOCK; NEW PROJECT is empty). The changelog's
"Gestures that moved" table lists every one.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.4.0/CHANGELOG.md). Every feature and how sure
we are of it on the unit is in
[docs/FEATURES.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.4.0/docs/FEATURES.md).

**How to play it:** the [user manual](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.4.0/docs/MANUAL.md),
[CONTROLS.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.4.0/docs/CONTROLS.md) and the
[cheatsheet](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.4.0/docs/CHEATSHEET.md).

## Load

| | cl6x | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default beat (A01), top line | under 30% | unit, 0.2.0 build (2026-11-22) |
| Heaviest free play, MAX | 84.3%, no clicks or dropouts | unit, 0.2.0 build (2026-11-22) |
| A01 then A04 (`scenario_factory`), peak | 26.5% | emulator, this build |
| Tracks 6 and 7 on WAVE MONO and WAVE POLY (`scenario_wave`), peak | 30.8% | emulator, this build |
| The ambient pattern (`scenario_ambient`), the worst case, peak | 32.2% | emulator, this build |
| Sample audition streaming from the card (`scenario_audition`), peak | 6.7% | emulator, this build |

All 41 emulator scenarios pass on this build (2026-11-26, cl6x). The pad gestures (stutter, LOCK) cannot
be played in the emulator, which cannot press pads.

The target is 60% for the worst case. Heavy play with 0.2.0 on the unit went past it without an
overrun, and the CPU guard steps in before 100%: it sheds POLY voices (VA, WAVE and FM4) and
thins GRAIN.

## Known limits

- **Tested on one unit**, with the cl6x build. On the 0.2.0 build (2026-11-22) the load stayed under 30% on the default beat, and MAX peaked at 84.3% in long, heavy play with no clicks or dropouts; a build from main boots on Enaga 0.3.0 (2026-11-25), but 0.4.0's load is not measured on the unit yet. Nothing new in 0.4.0 is checked
  on the unit yet. Every other load figure in
  [docs/BUDGET.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.4.0/docs/BUDGET.md) is from the
  emulator, which models no caches.
- **Older projects change a little:** on VA and WAVE tracks the insert filter loads as the nearest
  TON (its drive and resonance are dropped), and the GROWL preset is quieter.
- **WAVE and FM4 voices** share the budget of **12 POLY voices** with VA POLY, across all
  tracks. That budget and the CPU guard's thresholds are provisional.
- **The performance mixer, the looper, LOCK and LIVE RND are not saved**, and the looper borrows
  RESAMPLE's buffer: RESAMPLE and FREEZE wait while a loop is held. No overdub yet. One effect at a
  time.
- **A streamed sample** plays forward only: no START, slices or reverse. FREEZE is not saved.
- **MIDI clock, SYNC and USB MIDI** are lightly tested. (Enaga 0.3.0 fixes the DIN MIDI OUT stall
  with USB connected to a computer that is not reading.)
- **Before 1.0, nothing is promised** about the project file format or the controls.

## Reporting problems

not contact Sonicware support about STRATA or Enaga. Problems in Enaga itself belong at
[seawaffle/enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/issues).
