**STRATA 0.6.0** is an 8-track techno performance groovebox for the **Sonicware SmplTrek**,
built as an app for [Enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga). It adds the arrangement (song
sections, ARR REC and Track Launch), a live layer (ENERGY, TRANSPOSE, VariSpeed, tap tempo),
JAM REC to the card, the line input on the mixer, your own sounds, WAVE scan modes, stereo
width, a chorus and 16th-note triplets to
[0.5.0](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/releases/tag/v0.5.0), on the same Enaga 0.4.0.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **0.6.0 needs Enaga 0.4.0 firmware**, the same as 0.5.0, and the zip **does not include it**.
> Updating from 0.5.0 is the app alone. From 0.4.0 or earlier, install Enaga 0.4.0 from
> [Enaga's own release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.4.0) first. The
> [card builder](https://strata.einblick.dev) does both in one zip.

> [!NOTE]
> **Some gestures moved.** FUNC + turn VALUE is now VariSpeed, FUNC + SEL< / SEL> is TRANSPOSE,
> FUNC + push D in PERFORM kills all three bands (LIVE RND picks its tracks with FUNC + lower
> pad), and TRACK SPEED, TRACK PLAY, FREEZE and RELOAD SAMPLE moved from the FUNC menu to GRID's
> track menu (hold OK). The [changelog](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.6.0/CHANGELOG.md)'s
> "Upgrading from 0.5.0" has the full list.

> [!CAUTION]
> **An early beta.** Nothing new since 0.4.0 has been through the unit walkthrough yet, and its
> CPU cost on the unit is not measured. Expect rough edges, and please report them. Installing
> third-party firmware is at your own risk, and neither Enaga nor STRATA is made, supported or
> endorsed by Sonicware.

## Requirements

- A **Sonicware SmplTrek** whose SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**. The Lofi-12 XT and CyDrums
  are the same board, but STRATA has not run on either.
- **Enaga 0.4.0, exactly**, from [Enaga's v0.4.0 release](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.4.0)
  (`enaga-v0.4.0-smpltrek.zip`). Enaga's loader checks only a number the versions share, so
  0.6.0 on older firmware starts and may then misbehave.
- An SD card, and a **second SD card** with an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware to go back.

## Install

**First, the safety checks.** Do not skip these: a hung image leaves the power key dead.

- [ ] SYSTEM INFO shows **BOOT 1.0.17**.
- [ ] A second SD card holds an official `SmplTrek.bin` from Sonicware, so you can restore the unit.
- [ ] Holding **HOME (F4)** while switching on reaches BOOT MODE.
- [ ] The unit runs on the power adapter **without batteries**, so a hung image can be
      power-cycled.

**The easy way: [strata.einblick.dev](https://strata.einblick.dev).** Pick 0.6.0, and your
browser fetches this release and Enaga 0.4.0 from their official GitHub releases, checks both
against their SHA-256, and hands you one zip: copy everything in it to the root of the SD card,
hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on, choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**, and switch on again. Updating
from 0.5.0, tick "STRATA only". A tick box adds ten starter projects, a sample pack made by
STRATA's own machines and a one-page quick start.

**By hand:**

**Step 1: install Enaga 0.4.0.** Skip it if the unit already runs Enaga 0.4.0 (it does when it
ran STRATA 0.5.0): the Enaga menu (**F1 + POWER**) shows its version at the top.

1. On [Enaga's v0.4.0 release page](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/releases/tag/v0.4.0),
   download `enaga-v0.4.0-smpltrek.zip` (not the lofi12xt or cydrums one) and unzip it. It holds
   one file, `SmplTrek.bin`.
2. Copy `SmplTrek.bin` to the **root** of the SD card, keeping its name.
3. Put the card in the SmplTrek, hold **HOME (F4)** while switching on to reach BOOT MODE, and
   choose **SYSTEM UPDATE**. Wait until it finishes.

**Step 2: copy STRATA.**

4. Unzip `STRATA-v0.6.0-smpltrek.zip` on your computer.
5. Copy `SD-CARD/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA` to the card as `/ENAGA/APPS/STRATA/APP.ENA`,
   replacing the old one when you update. Keep the folder names exactly. (Once Enaga runs, its
   menu's USB DRIVE page puts the card on a computer over USB, so the card can stay in the unit.)

**Step 3: play.**

6. Switch on. Enaga starts the last app that ran, or the only one installed; **F1 (FUNC) +
   POWER** opens the Enaga menu, which lists the apps. A first start opens the factory project:
   press **PLAY**.

**Updating from 0.5.0:** step 2 only. Your projects and kits stay on the card and open as they
are. **From 0.4.0 or earlier:** copy `/ENAGA/DATA/STRATA/` to your computer first (a project
saved by 0.5.0 or later plays its LFOs wrong on 0.4.0), then steps 1 and 2.

**Going back:** to 0.5.0, copy its `APP.ENA` back; it opens 0.6.0's projects and leaves out
only the new settings. To stock firmware, install an official SmplTrek update the same way as
step 1. Neither Enaga nor STRATA writes the SPI flash or touches the bootloader, so that
restores the unit completely, and your projects stay on the card.

## What is new

**The arrangement.** A CHAIN entry is now a **song section**: besides its pattern, repeats and
mutes it can bring in a scene, snap or ride the morph, set ENERGY, the tempo and TRANSPOSE, and
store an exact mute mask (push A on the entry). **ARR REC** writes a jam into the chain as you
play it, a pass at a time, with ARR UNDO to swap the old chain back. **Track Launch** (PROJECT
twice) lets one track play its part from another pattern while the rest carry on; **CAPTURE**
(hold OK) keeps what plays as a new pattern, and ARR REC keeps launched lanes too.

**The live layer.** **ENERGY** (FUNC + turn C in PERFORM) takes the whole groove from breakdown
to peak on one knob, thinning the weakest hits or adding ghosts. **TRANSPOSE** (FUNC + the
screen keys) moves the melodic tracks through the key, or by semitones. **VariSpeed** (FUNC +
turn VALUE) moves tempo and pitch together like a turntable, an octave either way. **Tap tempo**
on VALUE, a **nudge** for lining up by ear, and PERFORM shows its FUNC layer while FUNC is held.
FUNC + push D kills all three EQ bands.

**Recording and sound.** **JAM REC** records everything STRATA plays to the card as a 24-bit WAV,
for hours (FUNC + REC twice). The **line input** is a mixer channel with sends, duck and tone.
**Your own sounds**: save a track's sound under a name, load it in any project, and copy a sound
from one track to another. **WAVE scan modes** move the wavetable with each note. **Stereo
width** spreads the POLY machines' chords or doubles them in unison, a **chorus** is the third
send, and **3/2** is a 16th-note triplet track speed. The clap sounds like a clap, the factory
kit is rebalanced, the presets sit within a few dB of each other, and accents on the VA and WAVE
machines no longer tick.

**MIDI:** the song position pointer puts the chain on the right section, and PROG CHG OUT sends
each pattern change to a second unit or a sequencer.

**Fixes.** The CPU guard no longer thins chords that were never in danger (it read 40-60% on
the unit with generated parts everywhere). A project opens at rest instead of inheriting the last
one's ENERGY or VariSpeed; a song no longer rewrites the project's tempo; the clock no longer
jumps back after a tempo change; a LOCK no longer writes launched parts over the pattern; ENERGY's
ghosts skip conditional trigs; and a fast turn of VALUE keeps every detent.

The full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.6.0/CHANGELOG.md). Every feature and how sure
we are of it on the unit is in
[docs/FEATURES.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.6.0/docs/FEATURES.md).

**How to play it:** the [user manual](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.6.0/docs/MANUAL.md),
[CONTROLS.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.6.0/docs/CONTROLS.md) and the
[cheatsheet](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.6.0/docs/CHEATSHEET.md).

## Load

| | cl6x | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default beat (A01), top line | 15-28% | unit, a build from `main` after 0.5.0's features (2026-11-27) |
| Generated parts on all eight tracks, top line | 40-60% | unit, the same build, before the CPU guard fix |
| Heaviest free play, MAX | 84.3%, no clicks or dropouts | unit, 0.2.0 build (2026-11-22) |
| A01 then A04 (`scenario_factory`), peak | 23.8% | emulator, this build |
| GENERATE at full density on all eight tracks (`scenario_genload`), peak | 35.4% | emulator, this build |
| ENERGY at the top (`scenario_energy`), peak | 34.6% | emulator, this build |
| VariSpeed +12.0 (`scenario_varispeed`), peak | 32.2% | emulator, this build |
| **Everything turned up at once** (`scenario_stack`): generated parts on all eight tracks, the line input, REL ALL, LEN ALL and ENERGY at the top, VariSpeed +12.0, TRANSPOSE and JAM REC, the worst case, peak | **45.4%** | emulator, this build |

All 67 emulator scenarios pass on this build (2026-11-28), with cl6x and GCC-only (worst case
79.9% GCC-only, the build that does not ship). The pad gestures (stutter, LOCK, choke by pad, the
held-pad launch) cannot be played in the emulator, which cannot press pads.

The target is 60% for the worst case. The unit reads about 1.2-1.8x the emulator on a light beat
and 2.0-2.4x on dense parts, so `scenario_stack` may go past it on the unit: that is the
first thing the unit test measures. The CPU guard steps in before 100%: it sheds POLY voices (VA,
WAVE and FM4) and thins GRAIN.

## Known limits

- **Tested on one unit**, with the {{BUILD}} build. {{UNIT_LOAD}} Nothing new since 0.4.0 is
  checked on the unit yet. Every other load figure in
  [docs/BUDGET.md](https://github.com/Devko/STRATA/blob/v0.6.0/docs/BUDGET.md) is from the
  emulator, which models no caches.
- **Projects saved by 0.5.0 and later play their LFOs wrong on 0.4.0** and earlier.
- **WAVE and FM4 voices** share the budget of **12 POLY voices** with VA POLY, across all
  tracks, and a unison note counts two. That budget and the CPU guard's thresholds are
  provisional until the unit's readings.
- **ENERGY, TRANSPOSE, VariSpeed, the performance mixer, the looper, LOCK and LIVE RND are not
  saved**: a project opens at rest. CAPTURE is not undoable. The looper borrows RESAMPLE's
  buffer. One effect at a time.
- **A streamed sample** plays forward only: no START, slices or reverse. FREEZE is not saved.
- **MIDI clock, SYNC, song position and USB MIDI** are lightly tested. Under an outside clock,
  VariSpeed and a section's BPM are refused.
- **Before 1.0, nothing is promised** about the project file format or the controls.

## Reporting problems

not contact Sonicware support about STRATA or Enaga. Problems in Enaga itself belong at
[seawaffle/enaga](https://github.com/seawaffle/enaga/issues).
